YOUR CITY BUILDS ITSELF

  1. Houses grow outward from your tower on their own and earn gold.
  2. Pick a building on the bar below, then click or drag over your houses to convert them, for good.
  3. Research buildings bring discoveries: new towers, armies and money buildings. No tree: your picks shape how you play.
  4. A Warrior Camp raises soldiers. Order them to attack a rival; destroy its tower and the colony falls.

Rivals can attack from about two minutes in, as soon as their army is ready, and they hit back at whoever attacks them: get Crossbow Towers and Walls up early. Mountains stop armies on foot. Grow onto Ancient Ruins (violet sparkle) for a free discovery and Treasure chests (gold sparkle) for gold. M menu.
